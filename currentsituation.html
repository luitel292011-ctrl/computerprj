What is happening to our environment?

Our planet is facing several environmental challenges. Climate change, pollution, deforestation and the loss of wildlife are affecting people and nature around the world.

The situation is serious, but it is not hopeless. Countries, scientists, communities and individuals are working on solutions.

The Current Situation

🌡️

Climate Change

Earth's climate is becoming warmer and more unstable. This can increase heatwaves, floods, droughts and other extreme weather events.

🐾

Loss of Biodiversity

Many species are threatened by habitat destruction, pollution and climate change. Around one million species are estimated to be threatened with extinction.

🗑️

Pollution

Air, water and land pollution continue to harm ecosystems and human health. Plastic and chemical pollution are important concerns.

Major Environmental Problems

Some of the biggest environmental problems today include:

  • 🌡️ Global warming and climate change
  • 🌲 Deforestation and destruction of habitats
  • 🌊 Water and ocean pollution
  • 🏭 Air pollution
  • 🗑️ Plastic and other waste
  • 🐼 Endangered plants and animals
  • 🏜️ Land degradation and drought

Is There Any Good News?

Yes! Although the problems are serious, people are making progress in several areas.

  • 🌊 In 2026, an international agreement protecting biodiversity in the high seas entered into force.
  • 🌳 More natural areas are being protected and managed more sustainably.
  • ♻️ Countries are working together to reduce pollution and waste.
  • ☀️ Renewable energy such as solar and wind power is becoming an important part of the move toward cleaner energy.

These examples come from recent reports and updates from the United Nations Environment Programme.

What Can We Do?

We may be students, but our everyday choices can still help the environment.

♻️

Recycle

Separate recyclable materials from other waste.

💧

Save Water

Turn off taps and avoid wasting clean water.

💡

Save Energy

Switch off lights and devices when you do not need them.

🌳

Protect Nature

Plant trees and avoid damaging plants and habitats.

🚲

Travel Smart

Walk, cycle or use public transport when possible.

🛍️

Use Less Plastic

Carry reusable bags and bottles instead of single-use plastic.
